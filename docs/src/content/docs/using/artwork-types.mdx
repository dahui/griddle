Steam shows a game through five separate images. Griddle gives each one a tab.

| Tab | Where you see it | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Capsule** | The library grid. The tall tile you scroll past | Portrait, 600×900 |
| **Wide Capsule** | The horizontal shelves: recent games, collections, search | Landscape, 920×430 |
| **Hero** | The wide banner across the top of a game's page | Very wide, 1920×620 |
| **Logo** | The game's name, laid over the hero banner | Transparent PNG |
| **Icon** | Small icon in lists, the taskbar, and desktop shortcuts | Square |

A game can have any combination of them. Changing one never affects the others.

The **Current** tab shows all of them at once, with anything you have not customised labelled
*Steam default*.

## Capsule and Wide Capsule come from the same place

Both are "grids" on SteamGridDB, separated only by shape. That is why a size you pick on one tab
may not apply on the other. Griddle narrows your [filters](/griddle/using/filters/) to whatever the
current tab can use.

## Logos have a position

A logo is drawn on top of the hero banner, so Steam also stores *where*. Apply a logo to a game
that has never had one and Griddle writes a sensible position alongside it: bottom left, at half
size. Skip that and some games render no logo at all.

To change it, go to the **Current** tab, right-click the logo and choose **Adjust position…**.
That opens a preview of your logo over your hero banner, with two controls:

- **Anchor**, one of Steam's five positions. There is no bottom-right or top-right, because Steam
  does not have them.
- **Width** and **Height**, the logo's size as a percentage of the banner.

Nothing is applied until you press **Save**, so try the anchors freely. **Reset to default** puts
it back to bottom left at half size. If Steam is running the change shows up straight away.
Otherwise Griddle writes the position to disk and tells you a restart is needed.

## Icons always need a Steam restart

Icons are the one slot Steam cannot change while it is running. Every other kind of artwork appears
instantly; an icon never does. This is not a limitation of Griddle. The Decky plugin and every
other tool are in the same position.

Apply an icon like any other artwork. Griddle writes it, tells you to restart Steam, and it is
there when Steam comes back. Non-Steam shortcuts need one extra step behind the scenes, which
Griddle handles without closing anything. See
[Non-Steam shortcuts](/griddle/notes/non-steam-shortcuts/).

## Animated artwork

SteamGridDB has animated capsules and heroes, and they do work: Steam plays them in the desktop
library and in Big Picture. Griddle marks them in the browser so you know before you apply one.

## Tile size

**Size −/+** at the top right, opposite the Library and Settings tabs, makes the tiles bigger or
smaller, and the grid re-flows to fit.

You get it on every grid of artwork: each of the five browsing tabs, the **Current** overview, and
your [library list](/griddle/using/finding-your-games/). Each one remembers its own setting, so
heroes can stay large while capsules stay dense, and your choice survives between sessions.

## Looking closer before you apply

A grid of thumbnails is not much to go on when two capsules look alike. **Right-click any result**,
or press the menu button on a controller, to see it at full size along with:

- who made it, and how it has been voted on
- its exact dimensions and file format
- its style, language, and any notes the author left
- content flags, if it carries any

**View on SteamGridDB** opens the artwork's own page in your browser. That is where to upvote it,
report it, or find more by the same author.

Opening the details applies nothing. There is an **Apply** button in there for when you have
decided.
