## Installed and All games

The library has two scopes.

**Installed** is what it says: games with files on this PC. This is the default because it is
usually what you want, and it is short.

**All games** widens the list to everything Steam has a record of on this machine — typically ten
times as many. It is worth being precise about what that list is, because it is easy to assume
otherwise:

:::caution[It is not your ownership list]
Steam does not keep a readable list of what you own. "All games" is drawn from Steam's local
configuration, so it covers games you have launched or configured on this PC. A game you own and
have never touched here will not appear, and Griddle never calls it "owned".
:::

Games removed from your account — refunds, and demos or betas Steam has withdrawn — are left out.
They have no name and no artwork, and Steam no longer knows anything about them.

## Filter and sort

The filter box narrows by name as you type. **Sort** offers name, recently played, or most played.

## When Griddle matches the wrong game

Griddle looks a game up on SteamGridDB by its Steam app ID first. That is usually exact, but it
fails in two situations:

- **Non-Steam shortcuts** always fail. Their IDs are generated by your own Steam install, so
  SteamGridDB has never seen them.
- **Re-releases and remasters** sometimes fail. Not every Steam app ID exists on SteamGridDB, even
  when the game clearly does.

Griddle then falls back to searching by name, which is a guess and occasionally a wrong one. Use
**Wrong game?** above the results to search yourself and pick the right entry.

Your correction is remembered, so you only make it once.
