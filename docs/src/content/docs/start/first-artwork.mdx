This walks through changing one game's portrait capsule, the tall image Steam shows in your library
grid.

## 1. Pick a game

Once your [API key](/griddle/start/your-api-key/) is saved, Griddle opens on your library. By
default it lists **installed** games; the **All games** tab widens that to everything Steam has a
record of on this PC.

Type in the filter box to narrow the list, then click a game to open it.

<Image src={library} alt="The library grid, showing installed games as portrait capsules" />

## 2. Check what it has now

The **Current** tab shows all five slots side by side and what each one holds today. Anything you
have not customised is labelled *Steam default*.

<Image src={current} alt="The Current tab, showing capsule, wide capsule, hero and logo side by side" />

## 3. Browse replacements

The **Capsule** tab searches SteamGridDB for that game and shows what is available. Scroll for
more; results load as you go, and the count above reads *loaded of total*.

<Image src={browse} alt="The Capsule tab with the filter panel open and candidate artwork below" />

If the results look like a different game, expand **Filters**. The button on the right names the
SteamGridDB game they came from; click it to search for the right one. See
[when Griddle matches the wrong game](/griddle/using/finding-your-games/#when-griddle-matches-the-wrong-game).

## 4. Apply it

Click any result. Griddle applies it, and usually **your Steam library updates immediately**, with
no restart. Switch to Steam and look.

If Steam is closed, Griddle writes the artwork to disk instead and tells you it will appear the
next time Steam starts. Either way it says which of the two happened.

**Torn between two similar images?** Right-click one to see its details: who made it, its exact
size and format, its style, and how people have voted on it. There is an **Apply** button there
too. On a controller, press **Y**.

## If you want it back

Right-click the slot and choose **Reset**, or use **Reset all artwork** in Settings.
[What that deletes](/griddle/using/resetting-artwork/) is spelled out before it happens.
